Genie Broadcast Messaging

Prototype · P0 · synthetic

Design reference for business-user testing. Nothing here is connected to a real system or real service users — the environment "Bradford Court" and its Genies are made up. Purple OPEN Q markers are decisions we still need your steer on.

New broadcast

Limits: 50-character title, 200-character message — the working defaults, to confirm in UAT.

Message type

Requires acknowledgement
The person must confirm they've seen it. You get a list of who hasn't, to follow up.
For information
Just shown. No confirmation, no chasing. Delivery only — no read tracking (OD-2).

Recipients

Only deployed (in-use) Genies can be targeted. Not-yet-deployed ones are shown greyed and can't be selected.

When

Quick:

Time sensitivity

OPEN Q · OD-12 If a time-sensitive message expires undelivered, should that show on the staff list? (Quiet-hours interaction is settled: it expires during quiet hours too.)

Scheduled (not yet released)

You can edit or cancel a broadcast only before it's released. Once released it's gone out — no recall (OD-4).

    DECIDED · OD-11 No recall and no undo window. Safeguards: the review-&-send confirmation, and cancelling a scheduled broadcast (above) before it's released.

    Acknowledgements

    Acknowledgement-required broadcasts show who has / hasn't confirmed. “For information” broadcasts show delivery only.

      DECIDED · OD-10 Re-prompt / snooze is out of scope — an unacknowledged message surfaces once, then sits here as outstanding for staff to follow up.

      Quiet hours Portal setting

      Per environment. Broadcasts due to surface inside these hours are held and shown when quiet hours end — not dropped. Existing calendar reminders are unaffected (QH-1).
